# Wisp

A Dynamic Island–style widget for the MacBook notch. macOS 14+, Apple Silicon and Intel.

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/javohiryusupovv/wisp/releases/latest)** — download `Wisp-x.y.z.dmg`, open it and drag **Wisp** into **Applications**.

After that, Wisp updates itself: when a new version is out, a green ⬇ button appears in the notch — click it and Wisp downloads, verifies and installs the update.

If macOS says the developer cannot be verified: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Features

- **Now playing** — Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube / YouTube Music / Yandex Music in the browser, and more. Artwork (live video for YouTube), progress, controls, volume. Each app in its own color.
- **Telegram** — new messages appear in the notch in a Liquid Glass bubble (sender and text).
- **Prayer times** — official timetable of the Muslim Board of Uzbekistan, 15 cities, reminders 30 and 20 minutes before.
- **Timer**, **clipboard history**, **quick controls** (volume, brightness, dark mode, keep awake, microphone, screenshot, lock).
- **Volume / brightness HUD** instead of the macOS one, **charging** indicator.
- **Placement** — top (notch), left or right edge, on any display; adjustable size.
